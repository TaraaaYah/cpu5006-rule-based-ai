ALGORITHM 1: ORDERED IF-THEN RULE LIST (first matching rule fires)
 1. IF persons is 2 THEN unacc   # A two-seater cannot carry a family
 2. IF safety is low THEN unacc   # Low safety is never acceptable
 3. IF buying is vhigh AND maint is at most high THEN unacc   # Very high price plus high running costs
 4. IF maint is vhigh AND buying is at most high THEN unacc   # Very high running costs plus a high price
 5. IF safety is med AND buying is high AND lug_boot is at most med THEN unacc   # Medium safety, a high price and limited boot space
 6. IF safety is high AND lug_boot is at least med AND buying is at least med AND maint is at least med THEN vgood   # Safest, roomy boot, cheap to buy and run
 7. IF safety is high AND buying is low AND maint is high AND lug_boot is at least med THEN vgood   # Safest, roomy boot, cheapest to buy
 8. IF safety is high AND buying is at least med AND maint is at least med THEN good   # Safest and cheap to buy and run
 9. IF safety is med AND buying is low AND maint is low THEN good   # Medium safety but the cheapest to buy and run
10. OTHERWISE acc
